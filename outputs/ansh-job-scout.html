Quality-control audit

What changed in this scout

Search-result snippets are discovery hints only. A role enters the active board only after its direct destination is checked.

LinkedIn is split in two

LinkedIn Jobs and normal employee or recruiter hiring posts are searched separately. Exact titles are supplemented with fresher, graduate, apprentice, associate, decision science, insights, reporting, and operations-analytics terms.

More source families

Employer ATS pages on Lever, Greenhouse, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workday, and Taleo now form a separate lane alongside Internshala, Unstop, Hirist, YC, Wellfound, Cutshort, Instahyre, Foundit, Naukri, and Indeed.

Direct link still gates entry

Every active card has a checked destination. Cached results that looked fresh but redirected to expired LinkedIn searches or Greenhouse error pages were excluded, and closed IDs stay deduplicated in the archive.

Search method

How each run works

The workflow is designed to find fresh openings without letting low-trust aggregator noise dominate the list.

1. Discover by lane

Run exact and adjacent role families against every target city on LinkedIn Jobs, normal LinkedIn hiring posts, specialist portals, startup boards, broad job portals, employer ATS pages, and company careers. Use the past 24 hours first and past 7 days second.

2. Verify the destination

Open the final URL. Require a live apply control or a current named hiring post. Reject 404/410 responses, expired redirects, closed notices, blocked or unidentified forms, and snippets whose destination cannot be verified.

3. Canonicalize and rank

Deduplicate by platform job ID, normalized final URL, and company + role + location. Keep closed IDs in the archive, then rank the surviving roles by eligibility, CV fit, access, freshness, and source trust.

SignalTier 1Tier 2Tier 3
EligibilityFresher, intern, or 0-1 years1-2 years with strong overlap2+ years, degree/domain filter, or contradiction

Link gateNo active card without a direct, checked source URL; expired or unverifiable destinations go to the archive or are excluded.
