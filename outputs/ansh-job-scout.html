Search method

How each run works

The workflow is designed to find fresh openings without letting low-trust aggregator noise dominate the list.

1. Discover

Search LinkedIn Jobs, normal LinkedIn hiring posts, Wellfound, Cutshort, Instahyre, Foundit, Naukri when useful, YC/startup boards, and company career pages.

2. Verify

Open the strongest source, confirm that the role is accepting applications, capture date/freshness, and mark contradictions or expired redirects.

3. Rank

Compare eligibility, CV evidence, location, employer/recruiter access, competition, and source trust. Deduplicate by company plus role plus location.
